Wisdom Around the Table

Our Renowned Guests

Meet the visionary founders, seasoned investors, and creative specialists who have sat at Flow's Table to share their life journeys and business insights.

At Flow's Table, we believe that the best learning happens in safe, supportive, and open-minded spaces. Over the years, we have been honored to host some of the most prominent builders, tech executives, and financial mentors in the country. Here are some of the distinguished guests who have shared their wisdom with our community.

P'Mi Tiwa Shintadapong

President, Investors Association of Thailand

President of the Investors Association of Thailand and one of the country's most respected value investors, specializing in identifying structural economic bottlenecks and businesses with durable pricing power. He champions 'rational faith' — conviction built on deep primary research rather than crowd psychology. As a cornerstone of Flow's Table, he regularly shares his 'wisdom over information' mindset and guides the natural flow of dialogue.

P'Jekky Suthon Singhasitthangkul

Value & Crisis Opportunity Investor | Eclipse Fund Lead

A value-investing veteran with 15+ years identifying fundamentally strong businesses during market stress, panic selling, and valuation dislocation. Lead of the Eclipse Fund and creator of the proprietary 'Bottom-Fishing' strategy, he shares deep wisdom on finding value when others see only risk — believing that investing is the art of seeing value when others cannot.

P'Pom Pawoot Pom Ponpipat

Founder, TARAD.com | President, Thailand e-Commerce Association

Founder of TARAD.com, Thailand's largest C2C e-commerce platform (acquired by Rakuten in 2009), and President of the Thailand e-Commerce Association. Widely recognized as the 'Godfather of Thai E-Commerce,' he has invested in over 60 startups and launched ventures since age 23. At the table, he shares data-driven leadership strategies and hard-won lessons from building Thailand's internet ecosystem from the ground up.

Shark Jing Chanapun Juangroongruangkit

SVP, Thai Summit Group & Investor

Senior Vice President of Thai Summit Group, one of Southeast Asia's largest automotive parts manufacturers, founded by her family in 1977 and now operating across 8 countries. A prominent business leader recognized for driving automation and Industry 4.0 transformation in manufacturing, she shares insights on scaling a multi-generational industrial empire, the future of smart manufacturing, and navigating the global automotive supply chain.

Dr. Kid Supachai Parchariyanon

Co-Founder & CEO, RISE | Managing Partner, SeaX Ventures

Co-founder and CEO of RISE, Southeast Asia's premier corporate innovation accelerator, and Managing Partner at SeaX Ventures. A leading voice in the regional startup ecosystem, he mentors the community on scaling organizations through strategic innovation, building corporate-startup ecosystems, and the intersection of enterprise transformation and venture capital.

P'Toy Kasidis Satangmongkol

Founder, DataRockie & Data Science Educator

Founder of DataRockie, one of Thailand's leading data science education platforms, built as a solopreneur. A self-described 'generalist in practice, stoic at heart,' he has taught R and Python programming to thousands of students across the country. He brings a rare blend of data expertise, Stoic philosophy, and one-person-business strategy to Flow's Table conversations.

P'Tre Kittipol Pramoj

Director, Sammakorn & Racing Driver

Director of Sammakorn Public Company Limited, one of Thailand's leading real estate developers, and a competitive racing driver in the Thailand Super Series, racing a Ferrari 488 Challenge EVO in the Super Car GTM class. Also serving on the board of Tanachira Retail Corporation, he brings a rare dual perspective on calculated risk-taking—both in the boardroom and on the track.

P'Mek Nithi Satchatippavarn

Co-Founder & CEO, MyCloudFulfillment

Co-founder and CEO of MyCloudFulfillment, a tech-enabled supply chain and fulfillment platform for e-commerce businesses in Thailand. A Babson College graduate, he has successfully raised Series B funding and built one of the country's leading fulfillment services. He shares hard-won lessons on logistics optimization, scaling operations across multiple sales channels, and navigating the venture capital landscape in Southeast Asia.

P'Pek Punyawe Chantarakajorn

Professional Trader & Author

A professional trader, investor, and author of five books on financial markets and investing psychology. He shares deep expertise on trading strategy, technical analysis, and the discipline required to navigate market volatility. Beyond charts and numbers, he brings philosophical perspectives on wealth creation, business moats, and the mindset of compounding knowledge over time.

P'Benz Arnun Treetitiapt

Digital Entrepreneur & Solopreneur

A successful digital entrepreneur and solopreneur who pioneered self-funded educational courses in Thailand. At the table, he shares actionable frameworks on launching one-person businesses, designing high-margin digital products, and building active online audiences without external funding or large teams.

Shark Benz Vorapat Chavananikul

CFO & CSO, Boonrawd Trading | MD, Singha Ventures

Chief Financial Officer and Chief Strategy Officer of Boonrawd Trading (Singha), and Managing Director of Singha Ventures, the corporate venture capital arm of the Boonrawd Group. He also serves as Deputy CEO of Boonrawd Trading and Director of Singha Estate. With 20+ years spanning retail, consumer goods, and finance — including prior roles at Tesco Thailand, BCG, and Optasia Capital where he executed multiple IPOs and M&A deals — he shares powerful perspectives on corporate strategy, CVC investing, and steering one of Thailand's most iconic conglomerates through digital transformation.

P'Frung Narikun Ketprapakorn

Actress, Doctor & Content Creator

A beloved Thai actress, medical doctor, and content creator who shares her journey balancing a career in medicine with media production. She brings inspiring insights on discipline, mental wellness, and finding passion across multiple careers.

P'Kok Tanarat Ruckariyapong

Vice President, SAPPE (Mogu Mogu, Beauti Drink)

Vice President of SAPPE Public Company Limited, the Thai beverage powerhouse behind globally loved brands like Mogu Mogu and Sappe Beauti, exporting to over 100 countries. Part of the Ruckariyapong family that built the company from a small snacks business in 1973 into a multi-billion-baht listed enterprise, he brings deep insights on scaling a family business, FMCG innovation, and building a global lifestyle brand from Thailand.

P'Em Udomsak Rakwongwan

P'Peesamac Peesadech Pechnoi

Co-Founder, BASE Playhouse | Learning Designer & Technologist

Co-founder of BASE Playhouse and a learning designer and technologist specializing in creative marketing and innovation strategy. P'Golf Natthakorn Wiangin

Tech & Media Strategy

P'Shane Prachaya Mora

Managing Director, SERVgroup

Managing Director of SERVgroup, a Thai hospitality and lifestyle company. P'Phone Ruttanun Vilailuck

Third-Generation Leader, Samart Corporation

Third-generation leader of Samart Corporation, a Thai conglomerate founded in 1955 with 40 affiliates, four of which are listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand. P'Pat Naranpat Thitipattakul
